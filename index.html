01 / THE PLACE

A café that actually feels local.

Something More Productive opened in 2023 with a simple idea: serious coffee without taking itself too seriously. Local suppliers, handmade details and familiar faces do the rest.

MCR
TOGETHER

NO-EGOS ENERGY

Small place. Big personality.

The café has become a meeting point for students, families and regulars. Coffee is central, but the atmosphere is deliberately social rather than stiff or precious.
